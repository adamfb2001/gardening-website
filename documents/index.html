What we do

Everything your garden needs

One friendly team for the jobs that keep a garden healthy — booked as a one-off or on a regular schedule that suits you.

Soil maintenance

Aerating, mulching, feeding and pH balancing to give roots the rich, healthy foundation they need to thrive.

De-weeding & clearance

Thorough removal of weeds, roots and overgrowth by hand — beds, borders, paths and patios left neat and clear.

Planting & potting

Seasonal bedding, shrubs, hedging and pots — chosen for your soil, light and style, then planted to last.

Lawn & hedge care

Mowing, edging, scarifying and trimming to keep lawns lush and hedges crisp and tidy through every season.

Seasonal clean-ups

Spring wake-ups and autumn tidies — leaf clearing, cutting back and prepping beds so your garden's ready for what's next.

Regular upkeep

A reliable visit on the schedule you choose — weekly, fortnightly or monthly — so the garden never gets away from you.
